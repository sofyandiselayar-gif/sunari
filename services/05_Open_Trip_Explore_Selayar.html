DAY 01The hidden gems of Gusung Island

Pristine white sands, dramatic limestone cliffs & untouched coral reefs
  • Pick-up & briefing: morning group pick-up at the designated harbor meeting point, safety briefing and snorkeling gear distribution.
  • Balojaha Beach: expansive stretches of white sand framed by crystal-clear turquoise water.
  • Liang Tarrusu Beach: iconic limestone sea cliff with panoramic views, perfect for landscape photography.
  • Bone Malea Beach: leisurely coastal lunch with freshly cooked lunch boxes by the shore.
  • Liang Kareta Beach: secluded beach, open swimming and snorkeling over well-preserved coral gardens.
  • Return to harbor: scenic cruise back and return transfer to your meeting point / resort.

DAY 02The wonders of Bahuluang & Polassi

360° Nane Hill panorama, tidal sandbars, sea caves & Makam Karang
  • Morning departure: guest pick-up and transfer to the departure harbor.
  • Nane Hill (Polassi Island): light scenic hike to a 360° panorama of rolling hills meeting the open sea.
  • Pasi Timbul Polassi: the emerging white sandbar in the middle of the sea (subject to tides).
  • Island lunch break: freshly prepared warm lunch box on the island shore.
  • Liang Lipang (Bahuluang Island): exotic coastal cave with natural rock formations, arches and limestone cliffs.
  • Snorkeling at Makam Karang: legendary underwater coral mound teeming with tropical fish.
  • Documentation handover & farewell: return cruise, photo & video package handover and drop-off.
